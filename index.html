How it works

1

Open Lidl Plus → Account → Digital Receipt, pick a receipt and copy all the text.

2

Paste the text above and tap Parse Receipt.

3

Tap an item to select it (highlighted in blue), then tap a person button at the bottom. Selection moves to the next unassigned item automatically.

Usage & shortcuts

  • % button: split an item by percentage.
  • Normalize: scale shares to total 100%.
  • Name initials: assign and move down; collisions use the next available letter. C assigns Common.
  •  / : move between items and open sliders.
  • %: toggle percentage controls.  / : adjust by 5%.
  • ? or long-press an item on mobile: open Google Images.
